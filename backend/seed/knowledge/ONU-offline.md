ONU Offline Field Checks
1.0
## Confirm service impact
Check whether the reported loss affects one endpoint or multiple endpoints on the same access segment. Compare the service status with the latest network event before dispatch.
## Inspect optical path
Verify the patch lead is seated and inspect for visible bends or damage. Use approved optical test equipment and follow site safety practices. Do not disconnect shared plant to test a single endpoint.
## Escalate
If the ONU remains offline after local connection checks, record optical readings and access-node reference, then escalate through the network operations process. Do not factory-reset customer equipment as an initial diagnostic step.
