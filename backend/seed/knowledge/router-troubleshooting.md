Router Troubleshooting Field Checks
1.0
## Confirm router reachability
Check whether the home router answers management probes from the access segment. Compare its reachability with the reported service impact before dispatch.
## Inspect LAN and Wi-Fi
Verify power, uplink cable seating, and Wi-Fi broadcast state. Confirm the subscriber can reach the local gateway before changing any radio settings.
## Factory reset decision
Treat a factory reset as a last resort. Record the current configuration, confirm the customer accepts the reset, and re-provision the service afterward.
