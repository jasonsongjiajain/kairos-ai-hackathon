HSI Throughput Qualification
1.0
## Qualify the throughput gap
Confirm measured versus subscribed speed over a wired connection. A wireless-only shortfall points to in-home Wi-Fi rather than the access network.
## Test the local segment
Re-test at the router and at the access handoff to locate where throughput drops. Record each measurement with the test method used.
## Route or escalate
Escalate a persistent access-side shortfall to the network team. For in-home causes, adjust approved Wi-Fi settings and re-test before closing the ticket.
