HSI Throughput Qualification
1.0
## Qualify the throughput gap
Confirm measured versus subscribed speed over a wired connection. A wireless-only shortfall points to in-home Wi-Fi rather than the access network. Run at least three tests and record the best and worst results.
## Test the local segment
Re-test at the router and at the access handoff to locate where throughput drops. Record each measurement with the test method used. Use a single-stream and a multi-stream test to separate per-flow limits from aggregate capacity.
## Check for in-home bottlenecks
Inspect for an oversubscribed Wi-Fi band, a saturated uplink port, or a powerline or range extender in the path. Confirm the test device is not running background updates or backups during the measurement.
## Confirm the access-side profile
Compare the provisioned line rate against the measured rate before escalating. Verify the expected profile in the service record and check for any recent profile or firmware change on the access node.
## Route or escalate
Escalate a persistent access-side shortfall to the network team with the measurements attached. For in-home causes, adjust approved Wi-Fi settings, move clients to the 5 GHz band, and re-test before closing the ticket.
