Wi-Fi Performance Field Checks
1.0
## Confirm the reported experience
Ask whether the issue affects one device, one room, or the whole home. Establish whether wired clients are also affected; a wired-only healthy path points to the Wi-Fi layer rather than the access network.
## Survey channel utilisation
Record channel utilisation, neighbour access-point count, and retry rate on both bands. Congestion on the 2.4 GHz band is common in dense areas and drives most in-home complaints.
## Position and band steering
Confirm the router is centrally located and not enclosed in a cabinet or behind a TV. Move high-throughput clients to the 5 GHz band and, where supported, enable band steering.
## Recommend a channel change
Select a channel that avoids the busiest neighbouring channels and re-test. Avoid changing multiple settings at once so each change can be attributed.
## Re-test and document
Re-measure throughput and signal strength in the affected location after each change. Document the before and after measurements and the client device tested before closing the ticket.
