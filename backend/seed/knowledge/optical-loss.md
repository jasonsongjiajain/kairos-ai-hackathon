Optical Loss Triage
1.0
## Validate alarm scope
Confirm alarm time, affected service, and whether neighboring endpoints share the same loss condition. Treat a single endpoint alarm differently from a segment-wide event.
## Field inspection
Inspect connectors and cable routing for contamination, strain, or tight bends. Clean connectors only with approved materials and avoid looking into optical ports.
## Record and route
Record measured receive level, equipment identifiers, and actions taken. Escalate persistent or shared-path loss to the network team; do not alter provider-side configuration without authorization.
