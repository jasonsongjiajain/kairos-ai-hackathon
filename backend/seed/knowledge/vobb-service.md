VoBB Voice Service Checks
1.0
## Validate voice registration
Check whether the voice adapter registers with the softswitch and whether dial tone is present. Confirm the impact is limited to voice before dispatch.
## Check power and cabling
Verify adapter power, phone cabling, and the data connection the voice service depends on. A voice-only fault on a healthy data link usually stays local.
## Escalate to voice team
If registration still fails after local checks, record the adapter identifiers and escalate through the voice operations process.
