IPTV Streaming Checks
1.0
## Confirm the symptom
Establish whether the issue affects one channel, one set-top box, or all IPTV services on the line. Confirm whether broadcast and on-demand content fail together or independently. Record the affected channel and the time of day.
## Check the access path
Confirm the data service carrying IPTV is healthy and that the set-top box has a stable network link. A TV-only fault on a healthy data link usually stays local to the set-top box or its cabling.
## Measure stream quality
Record buffering events, packet loss, and the measured bitrate against the expected bitrate for the channel. Sustained packet loss above one percent, or bitrate well below the channel profile, points to a delivery problem rather than the display.
## Inspect the local device
Verify set-top box power, HDMI seating, firmware version, and multicast configuration. Reboot the set-top box once and re-test, and record whether the fault follows the device or the line.
## Escalate or close
Escalate persistent quality faults to the IPTV operations team with channel, bitrate, and packet-loss measurements. For local faults, replace or reconfigure the set-top box and confirm stable playback before closing.
